COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you contact us

Here are some common things students may want to know.

01

What is GYANI?

GYANI is a student-focused learning platform designed to organize Indian Knowledge Systems study material, revision resources and practice questions.

02

Can I suggest a topic?

Yes. Use the contact form to suggest topics, corrections, improvements or additional learning resources.

03

Can I report a website problem?

Yes. Select 'Technical Problem' in the subject field and clearly describe what happened.

04

Is GYANI available in Hindi?

GYANI is designed to support both English and Hindi content through the website language switcher.
