ABOUT IKS

What is Indian Knowledge Systems?

ज्ञान

Knowledge Traditions

IKS studies knowledge traditions that evolved in India through textual, oral, practical and institutional traditions.

चिंतन

Interdisciplinary Knowledge

Indian knowledge traditions cover diverse fields and should be studied through their historical and intellectual contexts.

परम्परा

Study and Preservation

The study of IKS helps students understand, document and critically examine India's intellectual heritage.
