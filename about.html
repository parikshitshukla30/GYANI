WHY GYANI

Making IKS easier to study, not merely easier to read.

Indian Knowledge Systems cover a wide range of ideas, practices and intellectual traditions. For students, the difficulty is often not the lack of information but the lack of a clear structure for learning it.

GYANI addresses this problem by organizing learning material into units, concepts, revision points and practice-oriented resources.

HOW TO USE GYANI

A practical four-step learning cycle

GYANI is designed to support a simple cycle: understand first, revise next, and test yourself afterwards.

01

Understand

Read the concept and understand its basic meaning and context.

02

Connect

Relate the idea with its historical, philosophical or practical context.

03

Revise

Use structured notes and key points to strengthen memory.

04

Practice

Attempt questions to identify gaps and prepare for academic assessments.
